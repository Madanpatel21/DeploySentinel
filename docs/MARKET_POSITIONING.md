# ReleaseGuard — Market Positioning & Go-To-Market Blueprint

**Tagline:** *Know the risk before you release. Understand every change. Protect every deployment.*

---

## 1. Category Definition

**ReleaseGuard** defines the **Release Intelligence & Deployment Risk Control Plane** category. Rather than replacing GitHub Actions, Argo CD, Terraform, or Prometheus/OpenTelemetry, ReleaseGuard sits directly above CI/CD, IaC, supply-chain scanners, and observability backends as the deterministic decision, simulation, and governance layer.

## 2. Value Proposition by Buyer

- **VP of Engineering / CTO:** Cut Change Failure Rate (CFR) and unplanned rollback hours while preserving release velocity — with zero toxic individual-developer surveillance.
- **Head of Platform / SRE:** Eliminate blind Friday deploys, catch table-locking SQL migrations before staging/prod, and compress MTTR via automated Release Replay and evidence-backed incident timelines.
- **CISO / Security & Compliance:** Enforce signed Deployment Readiness Passports, SBOM/SLSA provenance, and Separation of Duties with one-click SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / NIST CSF evidence packages.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

1. **Growth & Mid-Market SaaS (50–500 engineers):** Microservices + PostgreSQL/Kubernetes, frequent schema migrations, preparing for SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001.
2. **Enterprise Regulated Platforms (500–5,000+ engineers):** FinTech, HealthTech, and B2B infrastructure requiring audit-grade Separation of Duties, policy simulation before rollout, and cross-project dependency visibility.
